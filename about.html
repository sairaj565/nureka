What is Nureka and Why Active Discovery?

💡

What is Nureka?

Nureka is an AI Computer Science companion available as a Custom GPT. It acts like a computer science mentor that guides you through coding, data structures, and architecture through Socratic conversation, rather than simple copy-paste lectures.

🧠

Why Socratic Learning?

Reading definitions or watching videos is passive learning—you experience "illusion of competence" but forget concepts quickly. Nureka forces you to reason actively. By deriving concepts from first principles, you build deeper understanding and longer-lasting memory.

Why Nureka Was Created
"Nureka was created after realizing that memorizing definitions helped pass exams but rarely built true engineering intuition. The goal is simple: learn concepts the way engineers discover them."
— Sai Rajiv, Creator of Nureka

ChatGPT vs. Nureka Socratic AI

Why use Nureka instead of asking standard ChatGPT directly?

Standard ChatGPT acts as a solution generator—it immediately prints code and textbook definitions, which bypasses your reasoning. Nureka acts as a teacher—it holds back the code, asks guided questions, and helps you discover the concepts yourself.

How a Session Works

Starting your first guided discovery is simple.

1

Choose a Topic

Launch Nureka on ChatGPT and select a starting concept, or type a concept you are struggling with.

2

Get a Scenario

Instead of giving definitions, Nureka presents a simple, real-world challenge related to the concept.

3

Derive the Solution

Answer questions and explore constraints. You'll name the concept only after you've derived how it works.
